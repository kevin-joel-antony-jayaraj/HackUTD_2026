CBRE Challenge:
The Invisible Network

The Lore & Context
Behind every modern commercial property —from downtown office towers and retail hubs to high -density facilities like
data centers—is an invisible web of dependent systems. Facilities management, access controls, HVAC systems, lease
contracts, and utility connections all rely on one another to keep operations running.
When these systems operate in silos, operators remain blind to systemic risks. For example, a zero -day cybersecurity
attack on a digital control system or an unmonitored telemetry anomaly in a facility's cooling loop can quietly cause
severe hardware damage, breach tenant SLAs, and trigger cascading power outages before operators even recognize
the root cause.

The Prediction to Break

The Mission
Break the prediction by engineering a working platform that uniquely connects fragmented real estate data to tackle this
challenge. You have full freedom to choose your team's angle —whether that means predicting emerging disruptions,
quantifying financial risk,driving concrete actions to protect a portfolio, r pursuing a completely distinct approach.
Your platform must move beyond passive dashboards to provide actionable intelligence that helps teams prioritize and
resolve issues before catastrophe strikes.

What You Can Build (Open-Ended)
We do not dictate your technical stack or architecture. You might train predictive machine learning models on live
infrastructure telemetry, orchestrate agentic AI workflows that execute resolution plans, build self -updating dependency
graphs, deploy real-time risk-scoring engines, or build something entirely different. We want to see your team's creative
approach to solving this problem.
To earn top points, your submission must feature a clear, live -demoable, working prototype.
